# Roundtable Beta Tester Guide

Welcome — thank you for helping us shape Roundtable before public launch.

## What to try

| Area | Where | What to do |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Pray** | [/pray](/pray) | Submit a prayer request, react to others' requests, check back for team updates |
| **Discuss** | [/discuss](/discuss) | Start a thread, reply anonymously, report anything that feels off |
| **Moments** | [/moments](/moments) | Share a short testimony or read others' God moments |
| **Daily Drop** | [/daily](/daily) | Read today's verse and reflection; browse recent drops |
| **Can We Talk?** | [/can-we-talk](/can-we-talk) | Connect with a peer supporter (text chat) |

No account is required for any of the above — everything is anonymous by default.

## What not to expect (yet)

- **No voice or video** — counseling and peer support are text-only for now
- **Limited live counsel** — volunteer availability varies; you may wait in queue
- **Not a crisis hotline** — if you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (e.g. **988** in the US, or your country's emergency number)
- **Beta rough edges** — some features are new; things may break or change without notice

## How to give feedback

1. **In-app** — use Report on Discuss threads; react and engage so we see what resonates
2. **Direct** — reply to your beta invite email or message your Roundtable contact
3. **Be specific** — what you tried, what you expected, what happened instead (screenshots help)

We read everything. Thank you for walking with us.
